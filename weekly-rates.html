Daily & Weekly Transportation Questions

What can daily or weekly transportation include?

Packages can include planned trips to beaches, restaurants, grocery stores, excursions and other destinations based on your plans. Share your destinations, dates and expected rides so Top Dog can confirm a schedule and quote.

What is the daily rate for two people?

The daily rate for 2 people is $100. Pricing varies with length of stay. Confirm your dates, schedule and included trips when requesting a quote.

How much is transportation for three or more people?

Pricing for 3 or more people varies. Send your group size, length of stay, pickup area, destinations and expected rides for a custom quote.

Is there a fixed weekly package price?

Weekly transportation in St. Thomas is quoted based on your group size, length of stay and travel plans. Request a quote for your stay rather than assuming a daily rate multiplied by seven.

Are unlimited rides, airport transfers or a standby driver included?

Included trips, pickup times and any waiting time must be agreed in your quote. Airport transportation can be requested separately; do not assume it is included in a daily or weekly package.

Can cruise passengers book transportation or a private island tour?

Yes. Cruise passengers can request pickup at Crown Bay or Havensight for beach transportation or a private island tour. Send your ship, date, dock, passenger count and all-aboard time.
