Frequently asked questions

Before you book

For the fastest answer about a specific route, send your date, time, pickup, destination and group size.

Do you offer taxi service from STT airport?

Yes. We offer private pickup from Cyril E. King Airport to hotels, villas, vacation rentals, ferry areas and other destinations across St. Thomas.

Do you pick up at Crown Bay and Havensight?

Yes. Send your cruise ship name, arrival date, preferred pickup time, passenger count and plans for the day.

Is the ride private or shared?

Your booking is private for you and your party. It is not a shared public taxi run.

Do you charge a luggage fee?

No. Standard luggage is included without a separate bag fee. Please tell us how many bags you have so the right amount of space is available.

Do children ride free?

Children under 7 ride free with a private transportation booking. Please share the children’s ages when booking.

Can I arrange several days of transportation?

Yes. Arrange daily or weekly transportation in St. Thomas for beaches, restaurants, grocery stores, excursions and other destinations based on your plans. Send your dates, group size, destinations and expected rides for a custom quote. Confirm the schedule and included trips before booking.
