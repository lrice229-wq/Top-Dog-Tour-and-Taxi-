Cruise Transportation Questions

Where can you pick up cruise passengers?

Top Dog can arrange private pickup for cruise passengers at Havensight and Crown Bay in St. Thomas. Confirm your ship and port when booking.

Can we go from the cruise port to Magens Bay?

Yes. Request private transportation to Magens Bay and include your passenger count, preferred pickup time, and required ship return time.

Can we visit beaches, shops, and restaurants in one day?

You can request a customized route that includes beaches, sightseeing, Charlotte Amalie shopping, restaurants, and other stops. Send your priorities when inquiring.

How do we make sure we return before departure?

Provide your ship departure information and the time you need to be back at the port. Your requested route and return timing can then be discussed before the booking is confirmed.

Is the ride private?

Yes. Top Dog offers private transportation for you and your party rather than a shared public taxi run.
