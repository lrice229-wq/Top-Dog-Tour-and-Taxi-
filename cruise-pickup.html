Cruise pickup FAQ

Helpful answers before arrival

Do you pick up at both St. Thomas cruise ports?

Yes. Private pickup is available from the main cruise arrival areas at Havensight and Crown Bay. Confirm your ship and port when booking.

Can you take us to a beach and pick us up later?

Yes. Send the beach, group size, preferred drop-off time and the time you need to return to the port.

Can we book a private island tour from the ship?

Yes. Private tours can include beaches, overlooks, shopping, local food and photo stops based on your interests and port time.

Is this a shared taxi?

No. Top Dog provides private transportation for you and your party.
