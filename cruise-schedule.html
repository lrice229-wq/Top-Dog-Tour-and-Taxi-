Understand the port abbreviations

WICO = Havensight

Ships listed as WICO dock at the West Indian Company Dock in Havensight.

Top Dog meeting point: China King, directly across from Havensight.

Havensight pickup guide

CB = Crown Bay

Ships listed as CB dock at the Crown Bay cruise port.

Top Dog meeting point: Scoops & Brews.

Crown Bay pickup guide

Schedule information can change

VI Now notes that cruise schedules are subject to change. Confirm your ship, date, dock and all-aboard time when booking. Top Dog will plan your pickup and return window around the information you provide.

When a ship arrives late or guests are delayed getting off, your driver waits and coordinates with you by phone, text or WhatsApp.
