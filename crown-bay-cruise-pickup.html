Meeting point

Meet at Scoops & Brews

Top Dog’s Crown Bay pickup point is Scoops & Brews near the cruise port.

Look for the small light-colored kiosk with the red roof and Scoops & Brews sign. Your driver will confirm your pickup time and remain available by call, text or WhatsApp.

Delayed ship?

Your driver waits

When the ship or disembarkation process is delayed, your driver waits and coordinates with you.

Return transportation includes an extra time window before all-aboard time. Weather, traffic and emergencies can never be guaranteed against, but routes are planned conservatively.

Popular Crown Bay cruise-day rides

Emerald Beach, Magens Bay, downtown Charlotte Amalie, Mountain Top, Drake’s Seat, Sapphire Beach, Red Hook and private St. Thomas island tours.

Downtown food suggestion

Chicken Fry Restaurant

For passengers visiting downtown Charlotte Amalie, Chicken Fry Restaurant is a casual local stop that can be included with shopping or sightseeing transportation. Ask when booking so the stop can be worked into your cruise-day schedule and return window.
